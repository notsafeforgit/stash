# AI-assisted contributions

Tools and agents may assist with contributions to Native Stash. The contributor
remains responsible for the result and must be able to explain its behavior,
design choices and verification.

- Review generated changes before submission. Remove unrelated edits and
  unsupported claims.
- Disclose material AI assistance in pull requests, including its scope.
- Run the same relevant validation required for other contributions and report
  what actually passed. Do not describe planned checks or deployments as done.
- Preserve source attribution and license notices. Keep credentials and private
  library data out of issues, prompts and committed fixtures.
- Keep submissions focused and reproducible. Tool use does not exempt a change
  from the project policy or review.

Owner-directed agent work follows the owner's authorization and the repository's
normal development, validation and publication workflow.
